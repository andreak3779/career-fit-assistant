## 💡 Energy Openers (First 5 Minutes)

Plan to say something like:

> "Thanks for taking the time to talk with me today. I've been excited about this opportunity because [mention 1–2 specific things about role/company]. I'm particularly interested in [one aspect of their tech stack or mission], especially given my background in [your relevant experience]. I'm looking forward to learning more about how the team works together."

## 📞 Day-Of Logistics

- [ ] Test video/audio setup 15 min early
- [ ] Have job description open
- [ ] Have your resume open
- [ ] Water/coffee nearby
- [ ] Notebook + pen for notes
- [ ] Quiet, professional background
- [ ] Business casual attire (if in-person/video)

---

## 🎬 Closing Statement (Last Minute)

Plan to say:

> "I've really enjoyed learning more about this role and the team. I believe my [experience in X + skills in Y] would be valuable to you, particularly with [specific project or challenge they mentioned]. I'm excited about the possibility of contributing to [company mission / specific project]."

---

## ✅ Post-Interview Checklist

- [ ] Send thank-you email within 24 hours
- [ ] Mention 1–2 specific things you discussed
- [ ] Reiterate your interest
- [ ] Ask about next steps
- [ ] Update application tracker with interview notes
